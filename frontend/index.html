Why it is not a template

Two agents do not get the same video.

Recolouring one layout is a template with new paint. Every scene role here has several compositions that were actually designed, and the architecture is chosen per agent — by the model when one is configured, by a hash of the agent ID when it is not. Same system, different builds.

Colour with a job

Two colours are pulled from the logo and held to a contract — at least 40° apart in hue, at least 1.8 in contrast. Prose stays neutral, the agent's mark is primary, numbers are accent. Verified against 36 real marketplace palettes.

Cut to the music

The backing track declares its tempo, and every cut, entrance and caption is quantised to that grid. Motion lands on the beat because it is derived from it, not timed against it by eye.

Its own words

Copy, narration and the staged request are written from the agent's real profile and services. Prices and service names come from the listing, never from the model — inventing either would stage a lie.

The scene at the centre

It shows the purchase, not just the pitch.

A generic promo tool can put a headline over a logo for anything. What it cannot do is show how an agent on this marketplace is actually bought. The middle of every video stages that loop: the request typed into an x402 session, the 402 challenge carrying the service's real fee in USDT0 on X Layer, settlement, and the delivery arriving in whatever shape that agent returns — panels for an illustrator, a chart for a market feed, a report for an analyser.

The window is labelled SIMULATED, because the session is a staged illustration. The service name, the price and the protocol are not.

How a call goes

Four tools. One of them costs money.

  1. preview_spec — free. The whole plan: script, palette, scene architecture, the staged request. Read it before paying for anything.
  2. generate_pitch — paid. Returns a jobId immediately, because a render outlives the payment authorization window.
  3. get_job — free. Poll every ~15s. Returns the delivery when it is done: video, poster, duration, palette, and the full spec it was built from.
  4. retry_job — free. Payment settles on acceptance, so a failed render leaves you charged. This re-runs it at no extra cost. It is the remedy, not a refund.
